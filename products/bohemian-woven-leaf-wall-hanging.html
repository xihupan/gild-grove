Product questions

Which woven leaf wall hanging designs are shown?

The main photograph shows the multicolor style, while the alternate view shows a neutral design. Mention the colorway or share the product image when asking us to confirm availability. A mixed-color assortment is not assumed.

What material and dimensions should I confirm?

Request the material, overall height and width, and the measurements of the supplied design. The photographs do not establish fiber composition or exact dimensions, and those details may differ by style.

How is the wall hanging installed and packed?

Ask us to confirm the hanging method, any supplied fittings and whether other hardware is needed. Request the packing method and package dimensions for your preferred design; room styling is not an installation guide.

How do I request a wholesale woven leaf wall hanging quote?

Include your preferred colorway, estimated quantity, destination and target arrival date if known. Ask us to confirm MOQ, selling unit, material, dimensions, protective packing, samples and lead time for that design. Customization requires separate confirmation.
