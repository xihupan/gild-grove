Product questions

Are the LED light and batteries included?

No. Both are supplied by the buyer. The decoration itself does not require a battery; a separate LED light may require its own batteries.

Is the hat removable?

The chubby and jack-o’-lantern size references show removable hats. The water-drop reference shows a one-piece top. Confirm construction for your chosen style before ordering.

Can I mix styles or request customization?

Mixed styles and mixed orders are available for this pumpkin collection. Confirm quantities and packing per inquiry. OEM / ODM, logo, color, and packaging customization are not available at this time.
