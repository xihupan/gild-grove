Before you inquire

Useful details, clearly answered.

Have a product, quantity, or seasonal display in mind? Send us the details and we will confirm the next steps by email.

Can an LED light be placed inside?

Yes. A customer-supplied LED light may be placed inside both pumpkin collections. LED lights and batteries are not included, and the decorations do not require a built-in battery.

Can I request wholesale product details?

Yes. Please include the products, estimated quantity, destination market, and required delivery date so we can reply with the relevant details.

How do I ask about MOQ and packaging?

Send an inquiry and mention the product styles, quantity, and delivery requirements you are considering. MOQ is to be confirmed. Packaging details, sample availability and fees, and lead time are available on request.

Do you support OEM or ODM?

OEM / ODM, logo customization, color customization, and packaging customization are not available at this time. Mixed styles and mixed orders are available; final quantity and packaging requirements will be confirmed per inquiry.

Is the hat removable on the black collection?

Yes. The hat is removable on the black pumpkin styles shown in the current collection.
