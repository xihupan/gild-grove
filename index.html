Before you inquire

Useful details, clearly answered.

Have a product, quantity, or seasonal display in mind? Send us the details and we will confirm the next steps by email.

Can an LED light be placed inside?

Yes. Both pumpkin collections are designed to allow an LED light to be placed inside. Please confirm whether lighting is included with your order.

Can I request wholesale product details?

Yes. Please include the products, estimated quantity, destination market, and required delivery date so we can reply with the relevant details.

How do I ask about MOQ and packaging?

Send an inquiry and mention the product styles and quantity you are considering. MOQ, packaging, sample availability, and lead time will be confirmed for your order.

Do you support OEM or ODM?

Customization requirements can be discussed by inquiry. Please describe your logo, color, packaging, or assortment requirements so we can confirm feasibility.
