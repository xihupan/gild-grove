Before you inquire

Before you inquire.

Have a product, quantity, or seasonal display in mind? Send us the details and we will confirm the next steps by email.

What should I send for a wholesale inquiry?

Include product names or links, preferred designs, an estimated quantity, and destination country. Add a target arrival date if you have one. We currently reply by email.

Can I ask about several products or an unknown quantity?

Yes. Choose Multiple products or Not sure yet in the inquiry form and describe your interests in the message. An exact quantity is not required to send an inquiry.

How do I check MOQ, packaging, samples, and lead time?

Ask about the products and designs you are considering. Confirm the selling unit, minimum quantity, packing protection, sample availability and cost, shipping costs, and timing before placing an order. These terms are not uniform across the collection.

Are assortment and customization options the same for every product?

No. The pumpkin product pages describe their confirmed mixed-style and customization limits. For other products, specify the designs and requirements you want us to check. Sending a request does not confirm customization availability.
