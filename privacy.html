Privacy notice

This notice explains how information submitted through the Gild & Grove wholesale inquiry form is used.

Information you submit

The inquiry form may collect your name, company, email address, country or region, products of interest, estimated quantity, delivery timing, assortment notes, and message.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to wholesale product questions, confirm order requirements, and discuss MOQ, packaging, samples, and lead time. We do not use the form to process online payments.

Form processing

Form submissions are processed by Formspree for Gild & Grove. Formspree may process the submitted information under its own privacy policy and service terms. Our public inquiry contact is sales@gildngrove.com.

Direct email

If you contact us directly by email, we use the information in your message only to respond to your inquiry and continue the related business conversation.

Contact

For questions about this notice, email sales@gildngrove.com.
